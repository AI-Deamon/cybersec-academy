# Day 4 — Assessment Rubric
*Used by the instructor to assess the quiz + lab. Formative — guides improvement, not punishment.*

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| **TCP vs UDP** (quiz Q1) | Explains reliable/handshake vs connectionless; gives a use case for each | Knows definitions, shaky on use case | Confused |
| **DNS** (quiz Q2) | Names DNS as name→IP and why it's needed | Knows it resolves but vague | Can't explain |
| **Handshake** (quiz Q3) | Lists SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK in order and what each means | Lists them, wrong order | Can't |

| **Lab** (Tasks 1–3) | Resolved DNS; captured DNS query/response; identified handshake to :443 | Completed with minor help | Could not safely complete |

**Scoring guidance:** 12–15 = strong; 8–11 = on track; ≤7 = revisit before Day 5.
