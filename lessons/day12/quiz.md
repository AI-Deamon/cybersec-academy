# Day 12 — Quiz (exit, 4 questions)
*Formative only — reinforces, doesn't punish.*

1. **The two ideas that separate a security professional from a criminal are:**
   a) Speed and skill
   b) Authorization and isolation
   c) Which tools they own
   d) How much they know
   **Answer: b**

2. **A "range" is:**
   a) A weapon
   b) A safe, isolated practice environment you control
   c) The internet
   d) A type of firewall
   **Answer: b**

3. **Isolation matters because:**
   a) It makes scans faster
   b) If a mistake happens, it stays contained and can't reach production/internet
   c) It hides your activity
   d) It's required by law everywhere
   **Answer: b**

4. **Testing a website you don't have permission for, "just to help them," is:**
   a) Fine if you mean well
   b) Unauthorized access — a crime, regardless of intent
   c) A best practice
   d) Encouraged in training
   **Answer: b**

## Optional extension (discussion)
5. Why is *documentation* (step 6 of the methodology) not just bureaucracy?
   **Model answer:** it makes results reproducible, hands work to teammates, and provides evidence you stayed authorized/in-scope — which is what makes the whole engagement legal and trustworthy.
