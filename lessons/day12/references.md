# Day 12 — Reference Sheet
*Keep — this governs every hands-on lab from here on.*

- **Authorization:** test only what you own or have written permission for. Intent is not a legal defense.
- **Isolation:** keep the lab off production/internet (host-only/NAT VM or provided range).
- **Range:** your safe playground — target VM + attacker box, reused Days 13-15.
- **Methodology (7 steps):** scope → authorize → isolate → recon → test → document → restore.
- **Four guardrails:** authorized · isolated · documented · in-scope.
- **Never:** exfiltrate, deface, or pivot outside the range.

## Further reading
- Your jurisdiction's computer-misuse law (e.g., CFAA in the US, Computer Misuse Act in the UK) — know the line.
- Day 13: first tools (Wireshark/Nmap) used *inside* this range.
